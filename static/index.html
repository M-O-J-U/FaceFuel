    How a photo becomes a summary

    Each photo goes through the same pipeline. Every result keeps track of which model it came from, and areas no model can see are marked as not assessed rather than guessed.

    1. Locate

      MediaPipe finds 478 face landmarks and aligns the face; the eye band is cut from it. The tongue is located in its own photo.

    2. Detect

      A YOLO11m detector per modality marks visible signs such as dark circles, xanthelasma or a coated tongue.

    3. Describe

      DINOv2 turns anatomical regions — cheeks, sclera, tongue zones — into feature vectors.

    4. Grade

      A small network grades each detected sign, sampled 20 times with dropout to estimate its own uncertainty.

    5. Fuse

      Signs map to wellness areas; face, tongue and eye evidence is combined with weights of 0.40, 0.35 and 0.25.

    Face

    Dark circles, wrinkles, redness, dark spots, rosacea and eczema, plus skin and lip pallor.

    Eyes

    Conjunctival pallor, yellowed sclera, xanthelasma, pterygium, conjunctivitis and a drooping eyelid.

    Tongue

    Coatings, colour, fissures, geographic and smooth patterns, scalloped edges, ulcers and white patches.

    The research behind it

    FaceFuel is an independent research project by Abdul Moiz Muhammad. The models have not been validated against blood tests, and that is the next step for the project. Treat every result as a hint, never a verdict.
